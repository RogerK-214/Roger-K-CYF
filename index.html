How I got to CYF London

This morning I visited CYF London for the first time to take part in today's workshop. I wasn't sure what to expect, but I was looking forward to meeting everyone and getting stuck in. Here is how I got there and what the journey was like.

  1. Take the train from Tonbridge station to London Bridge.
  2. Jump on the Northern line from London Bridge to Stockwell.
  3. Walk ten minutes through Stockwell to the Bluestar Building.

Tonbridge Station

First, I made my way to Tonbridge station, my local train station, just a short walk from home. Tonbridge is a market town in Kent, sitting on the River Medway, and is the nearest mainline station to my village of Hildenborough.

Northern Line

From London Bridge I jumped on the Northern line, heading south to Stockwell. London Bridge is one of the busiest stations in the capital, serving both the Underground and mainline trains, and sits just beside the iconic Thames crossing that has spanned the river in various forms since Roman times.

bluestar-building

From Stockwell station I walked to the building where the CYF London workshop is hosted. It was a straightforward ten minute walk, though the rain made it feel a little longer — a typical grey London morning.
